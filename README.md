## 🌌 About OIJI Studio

**OIJI Studio** is a modern digital web studio focused on portfolio websites, digital yearbooks, school websites, custom coding services, and modern digital experiences. From visual identity to user experience, every product is crafted to feel **premium, clean, and relevant** to the current digital generation.

OIJI Studio is positioned not merely as a website provider, but as a **studio that builds digital presence** — designing not just web pages, but impressions, narratives, and identities that audiences remember.

> *"Technology should not feel cold and boring. A website is not just an information tool — it is the first face that builds trust."*

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 🎨 **Portfolio Websites** | Personal brand showcase for creators and students |
| 📚 **Digital Yearbooks** | Interactive, emotional school memory experiences |
| 🏫 **School Websites** | Modern institutional digital presence |
| ⚙️ **Custom Development** | Tailored coding solutions for any digital need |

**Work Principle:** Combining aesthetics, function, and emotional experience.  
**Visual Direction:** Futuristic minimal · Glassmorphism · Soft lavender · Neon purple · Clean startup vibe.

## 🌐 The OIJI Ecosystem

OIJI Studio is designed to grow beyond a single service studio — evolving into a **creative digital ecosystem** with multiple product lines and supporting units.

| Unit | Role | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| 🏢 **OijiStudio** | Agency & Development | Core business for website creation, custom coding, and digital experience services |
| 🖥️ **OIJIPanel** | Client & Project Panel | Centralized dashboard for project status, revisions, files, timeline, and client communication |
| 🔬 **OIJI Labs** | Experimental Projects | Exploration space for prototypes, new concepts, and design/technology experiments |
| 🎨 **OIJI Design** | Templates & Assets | Templates, UI kits, components, and digital assets for sale or reuse |
| 📖 **OIJI Learn** | Educational Content | Educational content, design insights, and learning for the community |
| 🚀 **OIJI SaaS** | Digital Products | Digital and SaaS products themed around yearbook, school, community, or workflow |

### OIJIPanel — The Operations Hub

OIJIPanel is positioned as a centralized operations center — not just a technical panel, but a **control space** that makes the client experience more organized and professional.

Functions include: project tracking · revision approval · asset sharing · payment status · progress notifications.

> *With OIJIPanel, OIJI Studio appears not just as a vendor, but as an organized partner.*
